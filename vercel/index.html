Get an alert when a web page changes, using ChatGPT

Restocks, price drops, new job posts and freed-up booking slots appear without warning. Puley is an add-on for ChatGPT, Claude and other AI apps that can read a public page and keep watching it for you.

How to set it up

  1. Add Puley to your AI app on the setup page and sign in once (free plan available).
  2. Paste the page into your chat and say what to watch, for example "Email me when this is back in stock."
  3. Puley checks the page on a schedule and sends an email or in-app alert when it changes.

Limits

Very fast sell-outs can beat any alert. Some sites block every automated reader. SMS alerts are still in development; email and in-app alerts work today.
