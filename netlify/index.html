Compare a product price across countries with ChatGPT

The same product can cost very different amounts depending on the country's store. AI chats usually can't open those local store pages, so they guess. Puley is an add-on for ChatGPT, Claude and other AI apps that reads each country's own store page and returns the local price with the link and the time it was read.

Try these questions

  • "What does the PlayStation 5 cost in Japan, Germany and the US? Show the local price and the link."
  • "Which country has the cheapest price for this subscription?"
  • "Email me if this price drops."

Setup

Choose your AI app on the Puley setup page, follow its setup button and sign in once. There is a free plan.

Notes

Local prices may include different taxes and fees. Some stores still block access, and Puley says so instead of guessing.
